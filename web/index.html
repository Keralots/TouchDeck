Glass wallpaper

Add pictures separately for each orientation. This browser crops them to fit and stores JPEGs on the device. Glass uses the old single picture or a built-in gradient when a gallery is empty. Show now pins a picture for the next boot. Config backups include the settings, not the pictures.

Daily downloads Windows Spotlight pictures once a day. Internal storage replaces the previous set; microSD keeps an archive. Both mixes daily and your own pictures.

Saving this change restarts the device. Pictures stay in their current storage.

Counts above 16 require a mounted microSD card. For each orientation, 12 take about 5 minutes. Swipes can stutter meanwhile; a thin line at the top of the screen shows the download.

Screen & light

Screen brightness, the night schedule, sleep and the status LED.

Brightness

day
--
--

Set to 0 to switch the backlight fully off at night; a touch then lights it at 10% for a minute.

Matching start and end times disable the night interval.

TimeThe schedule uses the timezone set on the Time page. Until network time is available, day brightness remains active.

Sleep

-

Without touch and without presence. 0 = never.

Shows the wallpaper gallery (Display > Glass wallpaper) in any theme. At night it uses the night brightness; the screen turns off when that is 0 or the gallery is empty.

--

Motion, occupancy or a person: any of them "on" or "home" keeps the screen awake, and its arrival wakes it, also after Off in the quick panel. A touch always wakes it; that first touch does nothing else.

Status LED

disabled

Allowed on this board: --.

--

The LED follows the slider live while this page is open.

--

Set to 0 to switch the LED fully off at night.

Matching start and end times disable the night interval.

Optional input_boolean, input_number or light: the LED's day brightness follows it, and the Light slider on the device sets it. Empty = not linked.

WiringDrive the LED through a transistor, not straight off the GPIO. A pin already used by the display, touch, USB or flash is refused.

Vibration motor

Allowed on this board: --. Not the status LED's pin.

--

A low level may not start the motor at all.

--
WiringModule VCC to 5 V, GND to GND, the signal input to the GPIO. The motor needs about 3.7 V to start, so 3.3 V is not enough for VCC.
